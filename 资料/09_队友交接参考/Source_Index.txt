https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-data-access

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12087966/

[论文] Evidence-based guidance on reflective pavement for urban heat mitigation in Arizona (Nature Communications, 2023)
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36972-5
