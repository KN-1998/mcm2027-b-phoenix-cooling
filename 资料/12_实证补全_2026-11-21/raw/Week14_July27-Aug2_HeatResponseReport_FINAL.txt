Heat-Related 911 Calls  
      
Phoenix Fire responded to 210 heat-related calls this week, more than the prior week and the 
highest weekly number so far recorded this season. The areas with the highest call density 
continue to be near downtown Phoenix and along the I-17 corridor between downtown and Bell 
Road.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (69), other business (43).  
• 167 patients (80%) were transported for additional care, including 50 to Banner 
University.  
• Cold water immersion was used 45 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 156).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 51 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 8/1, with 23 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 479 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 8/1, compared to 317 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 67% occurred outdoors, 57% involved 
substances, and 25% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

Weather Summary  
High temperatures were above the historical average throughout the entire reporting week. The 
coolest day was on 07/28 with a high temperature of 107°F. High temperatures continued to 
increase throughout the week. By the weekend high temperatures were at or above 115°F. 
Extreme Heat Warnings were in effect every day of the reporting period.

City Program Reports 
• Heat Response and Mitigation: 55 Community Organizations and all City-operated heat 
relief sites have received supplies to support heat relief efforts, including over 4500 
insulated bottles, 3500 hats, and 7000 cooling towels since May. 
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 216 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Neighborhood Services: 36 new cooling ordinance cases were opened, 21 open cases were 
resolved with positive outcomes, and 3 new air condition replacements were completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Park Steward volunteers reached 666 hikers during this reporting week, 
distributing 652 water bottles at South Mountain, Piestewa, and Echo Canyon.
