Heat-Related 911 Calls 
 
Phoenix Fire responded to 60 heat-related calls over the reporting period, marking a new high 
for weekly call volumes this season for the second consecutive week. Most patients were adults 
age 25–54. The highest call density was in the general vicinity of downtown Phoenix, between 
35th Avenue and 32nd Street extending several blocks north and south of Jefferson Street. 
Patient Location and Transport Information: 
• Most common locations: roadways (17), residential (15). 
• 42 patients (70%) were transported for additional care, including 16 to Banner University 
and five to Valleywise. 
• Cold water immersion was deployed 12 times (season total: 27). 
Heat-Related Deaths 
• Ten heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 6/13, with four of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix. 
• 117 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 6/13, compared to 74 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 88% occurred outdoors, 70% involved 
substances, and 30% were among people experiencing homelessness. 
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

City Program Reports 
 
• Innovation: The equivalent of 12,928 single use plastic water bottles was dispensed through 
14 operating stations in the smart chilled drinking water initiative. 
• Library: Multiple patrons reported positive interactions with library staff and navigators from 
Community Bridges, Inc., at the Cholla branch. 
• Neighborhood Services: Twenty new cooling ordinance cases were opened, eight were 
resolved with a positive outcome, and three air conditioner replacements were completed. 
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team volunteers distributed 258 
bottles of water and made 52 educational contacts with hikers at Echo Canyon on June 13. 
• Water Services: Enrolled 31 new homes in the low-flow program to ensure ongoing service.
