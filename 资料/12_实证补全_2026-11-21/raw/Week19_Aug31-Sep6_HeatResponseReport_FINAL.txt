Heat-Related 911 Calls  
       
Note: 911 call data are preliminary at the time of weekly reporting; weekly and seasonal totals are subject to change.  
Phoenix Fire responded to 31 heat-related calls, the lowest weekly call total reported since late 
May. Residents aged 65 and above accounted for a higher percentage of the overall call volume 
than in most prior weeks.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (9), private residences (6), business (5) 
• 24 patients (77%) were transported for additional care, including 5 to Banner-University, 
and 4 to St. Joe’s.  
• Cold water immersion was used 3 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 234).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 150 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 9/5, with 72 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 629 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 9/6, compared to 426 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 63% occurred outdoors, 49% involved 
substances, and 33% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• For more information: Maricopa County Department of Public Health Dashboard

Weather Summary  
Daily high temperatures were generally near the historical average of 103–104°F for early 
September, with a peak of 107°F on September 6. August 31 was an exception, with the high 
only reaching 91°F with rainfall in the area. No Extreme Heat Warnings were in effect.

City Program Reports 
• Communications: Produced 13 heat response social media posts that generated 59,276 
impressions and 746 engagements.  
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 163 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners.  
• Neighborhood Services: Opened 16 new cooling ordinance cases, positively resolved 14 
previously opened cases, and completed two air conditioner replacements.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Park Steward volunteers reached 1,143 hikers during this reporting 
week, distributing 785 water bottles at South Mountain, Piestewa, Echo Canyon, and North 
Mountain.
