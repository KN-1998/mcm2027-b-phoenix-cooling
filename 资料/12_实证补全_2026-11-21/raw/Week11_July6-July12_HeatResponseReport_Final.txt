Heat-Related 911 Calls  
      
Phoenix Fire responded to 150 heat-related calls over the reporting period, the highest weekly 
total of the season. However, approximately 20% fewer calls were reported than the same week 
in 2025, despite similar weather conditions. Areas of West Phoenix in the vicinity of 67th Avenue 
and Thomas Road had a higher call density than expected; other patterns in patient 
demographics, circumstances, and locations were generally similar to prior weeks.    
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (50), single-family and private residences (29).  
• 117 patients (78%) were transported for additional care, including 32 to Banner 
University.  
• Cold water immersion was deployed 19 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 67).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 23 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 7/11, with 13 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 282 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 7/11, compared to 228 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 88% occurred outdoors, 65% involved 
substances, and 26% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

City Program Reports   
• Communications: Posted 60 heat response social media messages that generated 95,795 
impressions and 2,909 engagements.  
• Heat Response and Mitigation: 42 community organizations have received supplies through 
the City’s heat relief support program in partnership with AllThrive365.  
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 168 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Neighborhood Services: 13 new cooling ordinance cases were opened, eight open cases 
were positively resolved, and six air conditioner replacements were completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team volunteers distributed 216 
bottles of water and made 15 educational contacts with hikers at Echo Canyon on July 11.
