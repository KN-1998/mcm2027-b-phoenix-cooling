Heat-Related 911 Calls  
       
Note: 911 call data are preliminary at the time of weekly reporting; weekly and seasonal totals are subject to change.  
Phoenix Fire responded to 121 heat-related calls, an increase from the previous two weeks. The 
highest call density occurred in downtown Phoenix between Roosevelt St., Grant. St., 7th St., 
and 19th Ave. The Laveen area also reported a higher call volume than typical.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (56), residential (25), business (20) 
• 89 patients (74%) were transported for additional care, including 17 to St. Joe’s and 16 
to Banner-University.  
• Cold water immersion was used 24 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 210).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 113 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 8/22, with 58 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 584 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 8/22, compared to 402 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 69% occurred outdoors, 48% involved 
substances, and 31% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• For more information: Maricopa County Department of Public Health Dashboard

City Program Reports 
• Communications: Produced 23 heat response social media posts that generated 47,476 
impressions and 1,247 engagements. Additionally supported nine heat response-focused 
media requests, including coverage in Outside Magazine of the Phoenix Fire Department’s 
innovative cold water immersion therapy for heat stroke patients.  
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 204 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners.  
• Trailhead Outreach: CERT volunteers distributed 192 water bottles at Echo Canyon on 
August 22 and engaged an additional 22 hikers with heat safety information.
