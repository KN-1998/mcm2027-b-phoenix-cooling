Extended hour and overnight heat relief sites: 24/7 respite center at 20 West Jackson St., Cholla Library, Justa Center, and UMOM.  
 
Heat-Related 911 Calls  
      
Phoenix Fire responded to 16 heat-related calls over the reporting period. The largest cluster of 
calls (6) occurred between downtown Phoenix and the State Capitol, of which four involved 
people experiencing homelessness.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadway areas (4), hotels/motels (2), residences (2).  
• 10 patients (63%) were transported for additional care; Banner University received the 
most patients (6).  
• Cold water immersion was implemented for two heat illness patients (season total: 9).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• Three heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 5/23, with two 
of those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 67 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 5/23, compared to 25 at the 
same time last year. 
• Additional case data will be shared once five cases have been confirmed countywide. 
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

 City Program Reports   
• Aviation: Opened one new heat safety ordinance case.  
• Communications: 18 heat safety social media posts generated 9,997 impressions.  
• Community Assistance Program: Fulfilled ten heat relief transportation requests, including 
assisting multiple families seeking relief at the 24/7 respite center.  
• Innovation: The Chilled Drinking Water Initiative had 14 operating fountains and filling 
stations that dispensed the equivalent of 13,216 single-use plastic bottles.  
• Neighborhood Services: One new cooling ordinance case was opened, one was resolved 
with a positive outcome, and three air conditioner replacements were completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team volunteers distributed 360 
bottles of water and made 103 educational contacts with hikers at Echo Canyon on May 23.   
• Water Services: Enrolled 55 homes in the low-flow program to ensure continued service.  
Partner Updates and Announcements
