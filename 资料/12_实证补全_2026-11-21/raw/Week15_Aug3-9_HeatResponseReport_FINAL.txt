Heat-Related 911 Calls  
      
Phoenix Fire responded to 103 heat-related calls, a decrease of more than 50% from the week 
prior. Higher call volumes than typical occurred in the vicinity of 44th Street and McDowell Road, 
and along Central Avenue between Van Buren Street and Indian School Road.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (39), single-family and private residences (15).  
• 84 patients (82%) were transported for additional care, including 20 to Banner 
University.  
• Cold water immersion was used 23 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 179).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 63 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 8/8, with 27 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 558 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 8/8, compared to 328 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 70% occurred outdoors, 52% involved 
substances, and 27% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

through the weekend. An Extreme Heat Warning was in effect every day of the reporting period.

City Program Reports 
• Aviation: One previously opened heat safety ordinance complaint was positively resolved.  
• Communications: 10 heat response social media posts generated 17,177 impressions and 
326 engagements.  
• Library: Served several families at the Cholla extended hour cooling center who were 
seeking heat relief because of storm-related localized power outages.  
• Neighborhood Services: 15 new cooling ordinance cases were opened, 16 open cases were 
resolved with positive outcomes, and 4 new air condition replacements were completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: CERT volunteers distributed 156 water bottles at Echo Canyon on 
August 8 and engaged an additional 19 hikers with heat safety information.
