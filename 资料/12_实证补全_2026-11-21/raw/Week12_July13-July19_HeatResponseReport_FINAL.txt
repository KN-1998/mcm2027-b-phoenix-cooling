Heat-Related 911 Calls  
      
Phoenix Fire responded to 71 heat-related calls over the reporting period. While this total was 
more than 50% lower than the prior week, it still marked the second highest total of the season. 
Of the 71 calls, 11 occurred within a one-mile radius of the intersection of Jefferson St. and 7th 
Ave. Nearly 75% of citywide calls were reported between noon and 8:00 p.m.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (20), single-family and private residences (14).  
• 55 patients (77%) were transported for additional care, including 17 to Banner 
University.  
• Cold water immersion was used nine times for heat stroke patients (season total: 76).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 31 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 7/18, with 16 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 321 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 7/18, compared to 280 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 88% occurred outdoors, 65% involved 
substances, and 32% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

City Program Reports   
• Communications: Posted 39 heat response social media messages that generated 46,327 
impressions and 353 engagements.  
• Heat Response and Mitigation: 42 community organizations have received supplies through 
the City’s heat relief support program in partnership with AllThrive365.  
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 125 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Neighborhood Services: 14 new cooling ordinance cases were opened, eight open cases 
were positively resolved, and one air conditioner replacement was completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team volunteers distributed 216 
bottles of water and made 7 educational contacts with hikers at Echo Canyon on July 18.
