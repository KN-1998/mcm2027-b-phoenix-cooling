Summary 
• The operational heat response season began on Friday, May 1, including the opening of 
extended hour and 24/7 heat relief sites, all cooling centers, and most hydration stations.  
• All heat response programs are reporting normal operations, and all indicators are 
following typical patterns for early May.  
• This is the first weekly report for the 2026 season and covers a partial week based on 
the alignment of the calendar and reporting cycle. The next report will cover May 4–10.

Heat-Related 911 Calls  
• Phoenix Fire responded to seven heat-related calls over the reporting period, consistent 
with typical call volumes for early May. Most calls occurred outside of the downtown 
Phoenix core, including two in north Phoenix.  
    
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Two patients were in natural/wilderness areas, two were in roadway locations, and three 
were from other areas. 
• Three patients were transported for additional care, including two to Honor Deer Valley.  
• Cold water immersion was implemented for one heat illness patient.  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• One heat-related death has been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 5/2. 
• 42 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 5/2, compared to 10 at the 
same time last year. 
• Additional case data will be shared once five cases have been confirmed countywide. 
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

City Program Reports   
• Communications: Mayor Gallego, Councilwoman Hernandez, and city departments 
participated in a heat season kickoff press conference at 20 West Jackson St. on April 30.  
• Community Assistance Program: Fulfilled four heat relief transportation requests for families.  
• Homeless Solutions: Outreach teams engaged with 83 individuals and provided water and 
heat relief supplies as needed. 
• Innovation: The Smart Chilled Drinking Water Initiative delivered the equivalent of 4,847 
single-use plastic bottles last week and quickly detected and repaired a leak at one location.  
• Neighborhood Services: Two cooling ordinance cases were resolved with positive outcomes 
and two air conditioner replacements were completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team and Park Steward volunteers 
initiated weekend heat safety outreach at Echo Canyon, Piestewa Peak, and South Mtn.
