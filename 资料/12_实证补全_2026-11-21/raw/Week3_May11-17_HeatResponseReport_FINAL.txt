Summary 
• The two hottest days of the year thus far occurred during the reporting period with 
temperatures reaching 109°F on May 11. Heat warnings were in effect May 11-12.  
• Visits to extended hour and overnight heat relief sites doubled from the prior week.  
• Communitywide heat illness indicators remain typical for early May.

Extended hour and overnight heat relief sites: 24/7 respite center at 20 West Jackson St., Cholla Library, Justa Center, and UMOM.  
 
Heat-Related 911 Calls  
• Phoenix Fire responded to 40 heat-related calls over the reporting period. 26 of those 
calls occurred May 11-12. Locations near 75th Avenue and McDowell Road had a higher 
rate of heat-related calls than normal.  
 
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadway areas (11), residences (11), convenience stores/gas 
station (3).  
• 28 patients (70%) were transported for additional care; Banner University received the 
most patients (6).  
• Cold water immersion was implemented for five heat illness patients (season total: 7).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• Two heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 5/16 (no 
additional cases were confirmed last week). One case has been confirmed in Phoenix.  
• 64 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 5/16, compared to 19 at the 
same time last year. 
• Additional case data will be shared once five cases have been confirmed countywide. 
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

 City Program Reports   
• Aviation: Opened two new heat safety ordinance cases.  
• Communications: Heat safety social media content generated 29,295 impressions.  
• Community Assistance Program: Fulfilled eight heat relief transportation requests, including 
helping a family with a young child access the 24/7 site and subsequent support at UMOM.  
• Homeless Solutions: Successful outcomes from the 24/7 site included an X-Wing placement 
at the Phoenix Navigation Center for a senior resident who had recently lost their housing.   
• Neighborhood Services: Four new cooling ordinance cases were opened, seven cases were 
resolved with positive outcomes, and four air conditioner replacements were completed.  
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team volunteers distributed 288 
bottles of water and made 46 educational contacts with hikers at Echo Canyon on May 16.
