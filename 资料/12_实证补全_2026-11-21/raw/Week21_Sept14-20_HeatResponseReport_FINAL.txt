Heat-Related 911 Calls  
       
Note: 911 call data are preliminary at the time of weekly reporting; weekly and seasonal totals are subject to change.  
Phoenix Fire responded to 27 heat-related calls, which represents the lowest weekly total 
reported since late May. Areas east of downtown Phoenix and in Maryvale that have had higher 
call volumes this season showed a significant reduction during the reporting week.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (9) and business (5) 
• 17 patients (63%) were transported for additional care, including 6 to Banner-University.  
• Cold water immersion was used 4 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 248).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 230 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 9/19, with 115 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 567 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 9/19, compared to 382 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 66% occurred outdoors, 55% involved 
substances, and 34% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• For more information: Maricopa County Department of Public Health Dashboard

Weather Summary  
The reporting week had an average daily high temperature near the historical normal of 100°F, 
but considerable variability. Daily highs were as low as 90°F midweek and reached as high as 
104°F over the weekend. There were no extreme heat warnings in effect.

City Program Reports 
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 189 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Library: Shared a successful outcome from a cooling center patron who progressed from 
homelessness to stable housing and employment over a 10-month period in which library 
staff created a welcoming environment and facilitated connections to key resources 
including computers, technical education books, and mental and behavioral health services.  
• Trailhead Heat Safety: CERT volunteers distributed 192 water bottles and provided heat 
safety education at the Echo Canyon Trailhead on September 19.  
Partner Updates and Announcements
