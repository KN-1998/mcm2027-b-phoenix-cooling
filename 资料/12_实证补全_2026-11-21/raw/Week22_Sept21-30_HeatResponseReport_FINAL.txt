Heat-Related 911 Calls  
       
Note: Cases under review at the time of prior reports have been resolved through 9/30 and are now reflected in the season total.   
Phoenix Fire responded to 10 heat-related calls for the reporting period, marking the lowest 
average daily call volume of the heat season. Six calls were for adults aged 55+. Midtown 
Phoenix had the highest call volume, with zero calls reported in the downtown Phoenix core.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: residential (3), transportation and roadways (2), urban 
environment (2), business (2) 
• 9 patients (90%) were transported for additional care, including 1 to Banner-University.  
• Cold water immersion was used one time for heat stroke patients (season total: 260).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 511 heat-related deaths were confirmed in Maricopa County as of 10/31.  
• 222 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 10/31, compared to 185 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 69% occurred outdoors, 60% involved 
substances, and 47% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• For more information: Maricopa County Department of Public Health Dashboard

City Program Reports 
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 289 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Library: Shared the positive experience of a resident formerly experiencing homelessness 
who built a relationship with staff at the Cholla branch over a period of nearly one year. Staff 
connected the resident to a suite of City resources as well as Community Bridges, Inc, which 
ultimately led to full-time employment, stable housing, and renewed optimism for the future. 
• Trailhead Outreach: Community Emergency Response Team volunteers distributed 192 
water bottles as part of heat safety education efforts at Echo Canyon on September 26.  
Partner Updates and Announcements
