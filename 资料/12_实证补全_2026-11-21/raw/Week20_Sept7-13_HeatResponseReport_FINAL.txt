Heat-Related 911 Calls  
       
Note: 911 call data are preliminary at the time of weekly reporting; weekly and seasonal totals are subject to change.  
Phoenix Fire responded to 66 heat-related calls, which was an increase from the week prior but 
below the heat season weekly average. Call density followed the typical spatial pattern with the 
highest densities in downtown Phoenix and extending north along I-17 toward the Loop 101.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (20) and business (12) 
• 52 patients (79%) were transported for additional care, including 16 to Banner-
University.  
• Cold water immersion was used 10 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 244).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 185 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 9/12, with 91 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 607 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 9/12, compared to 401 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 63% occurred outdoors, 51% involved 
substances, and 29% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• For more information: Maricopa County Department of Public Health Dashboard

City Program Reports 
• Communications: Produced 8 heat response social media posts that generated 7,950 
impressions and 322 engagements.  
• Community Partner Program: For the season, in partnership with AllThrive365, the City has 
distributed heat relief supplies to 67 community organizations operating heat relief sites in 
Phoenix. This included 2,000 hats, 2,500 reusable water bottles, and 4,500 cooling towels. 
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 149 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Innovation: Dispensed the equivalent of 16,400 single use plastic water bottles through 12 
operating chilled water fountains.   
Partner Updates and Announcements

September 30. Information about facilities available after October 1 for heat relief will be
