Heat-Related 911 Calls  
      
Phoenix Fire responded to 160 heat-related calls over the reporting period, marking a new high 
weekly total for the season. The areas with the highest call density were near downtown 
Phoenix and along the I-17 corridor between downtown and Bell Road. The vicinity of 24th 
Street and McDowell Road also had a relatively high concentration of calls.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (49), single-family and private residences (34).  
• 126 patients (79%) were transported for additional care, including 24 to Banner 
University.  
• Cold water immersion was used 35 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 111).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 40 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 7/25, with 19 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 388 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 7/25, compared to 297 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 77% occurred outdoors, 65% involved 
substances, and 33% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• Heat-related death data are updated on the Maricopa County Department of Public 
Health (MCDPH) Heat-Related Illness & Death Surveillance Dashboard

City Program Reports   
• Aviation: Received one new complaint under the heat safety ordinance.  
• Communications: Posted 40 heat response social media messages that generated 27,851 
impressions and 307 engagements; supported four media requests concerning heat safety.   
• Heat Response and Mitigation: 42 Community Organizations and all City-operated heat 
relief sites have received supplies to support heat relief efforts, including over 4000 
insulated bottles, 3000 hats, and 7000 cooling towels since May. 
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 184 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners. 
• Trailhead Outreach: Park Steward volunteers reached 644 hikers during this reporting week, 
distributing 567 water bottles at South Mountain, Piestewa, and Echo Canyon.
