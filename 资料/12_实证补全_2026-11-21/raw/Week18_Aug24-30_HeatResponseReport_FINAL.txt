Heat-Related 911 Calls  
       
Note: 911 call data are preliminary at the time of weekly reporting; weekly and seasonal totals are subject to change.  
Phoenix Fire responded to 126 heat-related calls, marking the fourth-most active week of the 
heat season to date. The highest call density occurred in downtown Phoenix between Roosevelt 
St., Grant. St., 7th St., and 19th Ave, following a similar pattern to last week.  
Patient Location and Transport Information:  
• Most common locations: roadways (48), business (15), multi-unit dwellings (12) 
• 94 patients (75%) were transported for additional care, including 23 to Banner-
University, 19 to St. Joe’s and 13 to Valleywise.  
• Cold water immersion was used 21 times for heat stroke patients (season total: 231).  
Heat-Related Deaths 
• 136 heat-related deaths have been confirmed in Maricopa County as of 8/29, with 67 of 
those cases occurring in Phoenix.  
• 605 cases were under investigation in Maricopa County as of 8/29, compared to 416 at 
the same time last year. 
• Of the cases reported countywide to date, 61% occurred outdoors, 49% involved 
substances, and 33% were among people experiencing homelessness.  
• For more information: Maricopa County Department of Public Health Dashboard

City Program Reports 
• Aviation: One previously opened heat safety ordinance complaint was positively resolved.  
• Communications: Produced 13 heat response social media posts that generated 17,431 
impressions and 386 engagements. Additionally, supported five heat response-focused 
media requests.  
• Homeless Solutions: OHS Street Outreach staff engaged 167 individuals, and distributed 
water and heat relief supplies as needed. Additional heat-related street outreach activities 
are conducted by contractual partners.  
• Trailhead Outreach: CERT volunteers distributed 192 water bottles at Echo Canyon on 
August 29 and engaged an additional 22 hikers with heat safety information.
