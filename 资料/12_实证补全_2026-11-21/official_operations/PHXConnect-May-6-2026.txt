PHXCONNECT02 City of Phoenix Employee Newsletter
We got an early taste of summer back in March, but now we’re prepping for
the triple-digit temperatures to stick around this time. The City of Phoenix is
committed to the health and safety of our employees in the workplace. That
includes your safety during the hot summer months.
Every year, steps are taken citywide to minimize employee exposure to heat-
related hazards that may lead to serious illness or injury. This includes
updating Heat Illness and Injury Prevention Plans (HIIPP) across the City. 
You can find your department on the HR Safety webpage. New this year,
plans will also be available in Spanish. Department HIIPPs are also being
posted on bulletin/communication boards for applicable employee groups.
Heat Safety for City Employees
Stay cool: Take breaks in shaded or air-
conditioned areas.
Stay hydrated: Drink plenty of water
and electrolytes throughout the day.
Look out for one another: Watch for
signs of heat stress in yourself and your
coworkers.
Check in regularly: Supervisors, please
ensure frequent check-ins with any
staff working alone.
Know the signs: Heat stress can
develop quickly. Symptoms may
include dizziness, headache, nausea,
excessive sweating, or confusion. 
If you experience any symptoms,
hydrate immediately and move 
to a cooler area.
The HIIPP provides specific details on department safety control measures, but in addition to
these items, general precautions help to ensure everyone's health and safety during this period:

We are proud to celebrate the first graduating cohort of our
new Phoenix Supervisor Orientation (PSO) program. A total
of 19 employees representing 13 City departments or offices
completed the eight-week training program! 
PSO exists to ensure all City supervisors share the same
baseline knowledge, skills, and expectations so we lead with
alignment, clarity, and impact across our organization. 
Supervisors come together for a half-day each week to learn essential tools and make
connections that support their success in the City. Newly hired or newly promoted City
supervisors are automatically enrolled in PSO, and a new program cohort begins each month. If
you have questions about joining an upcoming cohort, email PSO@phoenix.gov.
